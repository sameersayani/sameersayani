With over 19 years of experience in software engineering, I specialize in building scalable web applications, APIs, automation tools, and modern AI-powered systems. I enjoy transforming complex business problems into maintainable, efficient, and high-impact technology solutions.

I work across backend, frontend, data, and AI-driven product development — with a strong focus on architecture, clean design, performance, and real-world business value.
